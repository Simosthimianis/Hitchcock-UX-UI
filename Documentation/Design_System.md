# Design System

## Typography

### Playfair Display

**Role:** primary headings, editorial titles and section dividers.

The high-contrast serif style supports a classic cinema / editorial archive identity.

### Space Mono

**Role:** quotes, highlighted statements and curated fragments.

Its monospaced character evokes annotation, evidence and archival labeling.

### Bricolage Grotesque

**Role:** longer analytical text, gallery descriptions and contextual notes.

Used to balance the more expressive display typography with readable modern body text.

### Lora

**Role:** references, credits, citations and filmography-oriented content.

Provides a more academic serif tone for documentation-heavy sections.

## Color Palette

### Background

- Near Black
- Dark Grey
- Pure Black in selected areas

### Primary Text

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Off White: `#F2EBE1`

### Accent Colors

- Burgundy / dark red for suspense-oriented emphasis
- Dusty Rose: `#A77C7C`
- Pale beige / soft pink highlights: `#E3BEB8`, `#FFB4A8`

## Design Principles

- **Cinematic atmosphere:** the visual language should immediately evoke suspense and classic cinema.
- **Editorial hierarchy:** dense information is broken into strong headings, imagery and curated text blocks.
- **Archive identity:** typography and labels reference cataloging, evidence and film scholarship.
- **Visual contrast:** black backgrounds and restrained red accents keep attention on imagery and key content.
